# Community and Content Policy

> **Review draft.** This policy records Ascion's approved MVP moderation,
> enforcement, and appeal rules. It is not effective until the related safety
> tools work in production, product and legal review are complete, and the
> policy is published through Ascion's release-tracked legal process.

## 1. Purpose and scope

Ascion is a shared game world. Players may communicate and collaborate through
world, organization, and direct chat; usernames and profiles; organizations;
invitations; and direct-trade messages. This policy applies to content and
conduct on all of those surfaces.

This policy supplements the Terms of Service. It does not replace applicable
law or prevent Ascion from acting where necessary to protect players, the
Service, or third parties.

## 2. Community rules

Do not:

- threaten, harass, bully, stalk, intimidate, or repeatedly target another
  person;
- attack or demean people based on protected or personal characteristics, or
  promote hatred or discrimination;
- post sexual exploitation material, sexual content involving minors, or
  content that endangers or exploits a child;
- reveal or threaten to reveal another person's private or confidential
  information without permission;
- impersonate others, deceive players, manipulate trades, commit fraud, or
  promote scams;
- spam, flood conversations, advertise without permission, distribute malware,
  or post malicious or deceptive links;
- use offensive or deceptive usernames, organization names, tags,
  descriptions, or invitations;
- evade a block, content restriction, social restriction, suspension, or ban;
- post unlawful content or encourage serious unlawful activity; or
- encourage self-harm, credible violence, terrorism, or other conduct that
  creates a serious safety risk.

Context matters. Quoting prohibited content to report it, discuss it in good
faith, or document an incident is different from endorsing or targeting others
with it. Even so, players should avoid reposting harmful material unnecessarily.

## 3. Reports and blocks

Players may report messages, players, organizations, invitations, and direct-
trade offers using the relevant in-app action when available. Reports and
appeals may also be sent to **safety@ascion.space**.

A useful report identifies the content or account, where and approximately when
the event occurred, the reason for the report, and any relevant context. Ascion
may request more information and may retain a limited snapshot of reported
content to review the report, document a decision, handle an appeal, protect
users, or comply with law.

Reporting does not guarantee removal or an account penalty. Decisions are based
on the available evidence, context, severity, history, and this policy—not on
the number of reports alone. Knowingly false, coordinated, or abusive reporting
may itself result in action.

Players may block abusive users through the available in-app block action.
Blocking is separate from reporting: blocking limits further interaction, while
reporting asks Ascion to review conduct or content.

## 4. How enforcement works

Depending on severity, context, prior history, and risk, Ascion may:

1. remove, hide, or restrict content and issue a warning;
2. restrict social features for 24 hours, 7 days, or 30 days;
3. suspend the account for 7 days or 30 days; or
4. permanently terminate the account.

Minor first violations normally begin with content removal and a warning.
Repeated violations may move through progressively longer restrictions or
suspensions. Serious conduct—including child exploitation, credible threats,
serious fraud, or deliberate repeated evasion—may skip earlier steps and result
in immediate suspension or permanent termination.

A restriction may cover chat, invitations, friend requests, organization
features, direct-trade communication, or other affected social functionality.
Ascion may also rename or remove an identifier that violates this policy.

## 5. Automated systems

Ascion may use automated filters, rate limits, and safety signals to reject
clearly prohibited identifiers, obvious spam, malicious links, repeated
messages, or similar abuse. Uncertain content should be flagged or queued for
review rather than used by itself to impose a permanent account termination.

Ascion will not permanently terminate an account solely because of an
automated text match. Automated signals may support a decision when reviewed
with other available evidence.

## 6. Decision notices

When Ascion removes content or restricts an account, the affected player will
normally receive a notice identifying:

- the content or conduct at issue;
- the rule and material facts supporting the decision;
- the type and duration of the action; and
- how to request review.

Information may be limited where disclosure would endanger someone, expose
another person's private information, compromise an investigation or security
control, or conflict with law.

## 7. Appeals

Players may appeal a content removal, social restriction, suspension, or
permanent termination through an available in-app appeal action or by emailing
**safety@ascion.space**. An appeal should identify the decision and explain why
it should be changed. One appeal is available for each decision; Ascion may
consider a further request if it contains materially new evidence.

For MVP, the operator monitoring **safety@ascion.space** may also review the
appeal. The appeal must nevertheless be considered afresh and recorded as a
separate decision. No decision will be made solely by an automated system.
Where an appeal succeeds, Ascion will reverse the restriction and restore
content or access where reasonably and technically possible.

Ascion targets a first response within 24 hours for urgent safety reports and
within three business days for ordinary reports and appeals. These are
operational targets, not guaranteed resolution times.

## 8. Illegal content and emergencies

Reports alleging illegal content will be handled separately from ordinary
community-rule reports where applicable law requires different information,
notices, review, or redress. The final public illegal-content notice route and
any legally required out-of-court redress information remain subject to legal
review before this draft is published.

If someone faces an immediate threat or believes a crime is occurring, they
should contact the appropriate local emergency service or law-enforcement
authority. Ascion may preserve relevant evidence, immediately restrict access,
or contact competent authorities where legally required or reasonably necessary
to address an imminent and serious risk.

## 9. Changes and contact

Ascion may update this policy as the Service and its safety needs evolve.
Material changes will receive a new version and will be communicated as
required by the Terms of Service and applicable law.

Questions, reports, and appeals may be sent to **safety@ascion.space**.
